# Design System Specification — GIBOR06 STUDIO (Editorial / Brutalist Folio 2026)

## 1. Visual Direction: Editorial / Brutalist Architectural Monograph

The redesigned portfolio for **Trần Gia Bảo (`gibor06`)** is conceived as an international architectural & product design monograph printed on warm ivory paper — immediately evoking the presence of an engineering & design studio with refined taste rather than a student resume template:

- **Zero Stock / AI Photos**: Built exclusively from monumental serif typography, exposed structural hairlines, color-blocked planes, and abstract Bauhaus/Swiss vector geometry (golden-ratio circles, orthographic projections, and structural matrices).
- **Newspaper / Monograph Masthead**:
  `"GIBOR06 STUDIO" | "DIGITAL PRODUCT DESIGN • FULL-STACK ARCHITECTURE • AI" | "SAIGON, VIETNAM" | "FOLIO // 2026"`
- **4-Theme Architectural Palette Switcher (`Alabaster`, `Cobalt`, `Terracotta`, `Travertine`)**:
  1. `Alabaster` (`data-theme="alabaster"`, default): Warm Ivory `#F6F3EC`, Deep Prussian Navy ink `#0F1E36`, Cobalt `#143A8A`, and Terracotta `#D9532F`.
  2. `Cobalt` (`data-theme="cobalt"`): Deep Architectural Cobalt `#102A63` & `#16367D`, Warm Ivory ink `#F6F3EC`, and Terracotta `#FF6B47`.
  3. `Terracotta` (`data-theme="terracotta"`): Warm Fired Clay `#F2E5DC`, Deep Espresso ink `#23140F`, Vermilion `#C84320`, and Cobalt `#143A8A`.
  4. `Travertine` (`data-theme="travertine"`): Roman Travertine Limestone `#E6DFD1`, Basalt ink `#181A1B`, Cobalt `#143A8A`, and Terracotta `#C84320`.

## 3. Page Structure & Content Manifesto

1. **Hero (`#studio`)**:
   - Print masthead, `"SOFTWARE ENGINEER — UI/UX & SYSTEMS ARCHITECTURE"` label, monumental serif `"Trần Gia Bảo"`, central manifesto `"Where human-centered UI/UX meets resilient Full-Stack engineering."`, philosophical studio description, 4-theme selector, abstract vector projection, and the 4-cell **`ARCHITECTURE & UX MATRIX`**.

   - Philosophical manifesto on software as inhabited architecture and the synthesis of human-centered UI/UX, multi-tier engineering, and algorithmic research at HUIT (zero CV minutiae, zero GPA/grade numbers, zero long skill checklists).

   - Four numbered capability pillars (`01 // UI/UX & Systems Architecture`, `02 // Full-Stack Engineering`, `03 // Data & Infrastructure`, `04 // Applied AI & Algorithms`), each with a monumental heading, concise philosophical statement, and geometric SVG emblem.
4. **Selected Works (`03 // SELECTED WORKS — #works`)**:
   - Four curated signature works presented with radical minimalism (title, single-line architectural description, 3 short conceptual tags, and color-block index).
5. **Research (`04 // RESEARCH — #research`)**:
   - Declarative presentation of co-authored peer-reviewed research at prestigious national scientific conferences in combinatorial optimization, high-utility pattern discovery, and deep semantic representation.
6. **Contact (`05 // CONTACT — #contact`)**:
