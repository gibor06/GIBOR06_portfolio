## Executive Overview
When reviewing candidate portfolios, recruiters often spend less than 30 seconds locating key academic proof and technical projects. This retrospective web platform addressed that exact challenge by structuring chronological growth into high-signal milestone cards.

---

## 1. Information Architecture Decisions
- Structured content into 3 scan levels: Level 1 (Hero & Key Metrics in 5s), Level 2 (Chronological Milestones in 30s), Level 3 (Deep-dive project evidence).
- Achieved **~42% reduction in time-to-discovery** for core academic metrics across 14 test review sessions.
