## Executive Overview
Online social communities and modern e-commerce platforms suffer from toxic user-generated content and unoptimized transaction patterns. This research combines **transformer-based Vietnamese NLP (PhoBERT)** for granular toxicity detection with **High-Utility Itemset Mining (EMHUN)** to discover high-value transactional patterns under constrained computational budgets.

The research achieved:
- **0.89 F1-Score** on internal Vietnamese toxic comment validation datasets.
- **~31% reduction** in mining runtime compared to traditional baseline utility mining algorithms.
- **Official Selection as Finalist** in the HUIT University-Level Student Scientific Research Competition (2025–2026) and **2nd Prize** at Faculty Level (2024–2025).

---

## 1. Problem Statement & Context
- **Linguistic Nuances in Vietnamese**: Vietnamese text contains rich diacritics, compound words, teen codes, and contextual sarcasm that standard multilingual models (mBERT, XLM-R) often misclassify.
- **High-Utility Bottlenecks**: Mining profitable transactional itemsets often leads to combinatorial explosions in search spaces when candidate utilities are calculated naively.

---

## 2. My Specific Contributions
- Preprocessed and normalized raw Vietnamese text using custom regex tokenizers, accent restoration pipelines, and stopword filters tailored for social comment datasets.
- Fine-tuned **PhoBERT (base & large)** with PyTorch and Hugging Face Transformers using weighted cross-entropy loss to address class imbalance.
- Benchmarked the **EMHUN (Efficient Mining of High-Utility Itemsets with Negative Unit Profits)** algorithm against standard baseline implementations on transaction data.
- Prepared comprehensive research documentation, ablation tables, and defended the findings before the Faculty & University Scientific Committees.

## 5. Key Learnings & Engineering Takeaways
1. **Domain-Specific Tokenization Matters**: Monolingual tokenizers trained on syllable-level Vietnamese tokenization (such as PhoBERT's Byte-Pair Encoding) outperform general multilingual models on nuanced sentiment and toxicity.
2. **Pruning Strategy**: Utility upper-bounding in data mining provides substantial pruning power without sacrificing completeness.
